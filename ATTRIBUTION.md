# Attribution

## UCLA synthetic rPPG avatar demonstration

The file `test-assets/ucla-avatar-1.mp4` is a transformed test excerpt derived from the public demonstration at:

- Research page: http://visual.ee.ucla.edu/rppg_avatars.htm/
- Original demonstration: http://visual.ee.ucla.edu/assets/images/publications/avatars_video_demo.gif
- Referenced work: Wang et al., UCLA Vision Lab, rPPG avatar / synthetic-video research.

Transformation performed on 2026-11-06: the first 144×173 cell of the 576×518 animated figure was cropped, upscaled with Lanczos to 576×692, retimed to 30 fps, and encoded as H.264 for a 51.2-second SmartSpectra input test. The figure is a synthetic avatar, not a human participant.

The UCLA page exposes the sample for research demonstration but does not state a separate open-media license. No ownership or permission beyond the page’s research context is claimed. Aura does not bundle the full UCLA dataset, ground-truth signals, or any human subject data. If the rights holder requests removal, the replay asset can be replaced with a self-recorded consented clip without changing the app.
